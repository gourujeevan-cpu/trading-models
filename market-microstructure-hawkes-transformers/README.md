# Market Microstructure: Hawkes Processes, ACD Models & Transformers

A collection of research notebooks exploring high-frequency financial modelling, event dynamics, volatility, order-book imbalance, duration models, and transformer-based forecasting.

## Notebooks

1. **ACD Modelling** — autoregressive conditional duration modelling for transaction/event timing.
2. **Hawkes: Market Events** — self-exciting point-process modelling of market-event arrivals.
3. **Hawkes: Mid-Price Jumps** — event clustering around mid-price changes.
4. **Hawkes: Order Book Imbalance** — Hawkes-process analysis of order-book imbalance dynamics.
5. **Hawkes: Volatility Bursts** — modelling clustered volatility events with self-exciting processes.
6. **Transformer: Volatility Prediction** — transformer-based volatility forecasting.
7. **Transformer: Gold Prediction** — transformer model applied to gold-market prediction.
8. **Transformer + ACD** — combining duration-style modelling ideas with transformer architectures.
9. **Multivariate Hawkes** — multivariate self- and cross-excitation across market-event types.

## Topics

- Market microstructure
- High-frequency finance
- Hawkes processes
- Point processes
- Autoregressive Conditional Duration (ACD)
- Order-book imbalance
- Volatility modelling
- Transformers
- Financial time-series forecasting
